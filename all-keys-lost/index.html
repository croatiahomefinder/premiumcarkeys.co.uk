Car locked or open?

Tell us which when you get in touch. It changes what we bring and how long the job takes.

Car is locked

We open the car, then make and program the new keys. Usually a mobile job, since the car cannot be driven to us.

Car is open

We go straight to making and programming the new keys, at your address or at the workshop if the car can be brought in.

How it works

  1. Send your registration

    And a photo of the start button or key slot, so we can identify the system.

  2. Get a fixed price

    Including any travel charge if we come to you.

  3. Prove ownership

    Photo ID and the V5C or other proof of ownership. We will not start without them.

  4. New keys working

    Keys cut, programmed and tested, and the lost keys removed where the system allows.

Questions

Can you do it at my house?

Yes, anywhere within about 30 miles of our Halesowen workshop across the West Midlands. A travel charge is added and confirmed before we set off.

I am more than 30 miles away. Can you still help?

Our mobile service stops at about 30 miles. If you can get the car transported to the workshop, we can do the job there.

Will the lost keys still work if someone finds them?

Where the system allows, we remove the lost keys from the car so they will not start it.

I do not have the V5C. What else counts as proof?

Send us what you have on WhatsApp before booking and we will tell you whether it is enough.

Should I add CAN Phantom while you are there?

You can. It is £399 instead of £449 when fitted at the same appointment.
