Volvo key questions

Do I need to go to a Volvo dealer?

No. We cut, program and test keys for supported 2010 to 2025 models at our Halesowen workshop or at your address within 30 miles.

How much is an extra key?

Each extra key at the same visit is £99. A second spare now means you are never down to your last key.

What keys do you supply?

OE specification keys, cut and programmed to your car, with a 1 year warranty. We do not supply cheap clone keys.

Will my existing key keep working?

Yes. A spare key is added alongside the keys you already have, so bring every working key to the appointment.

What does all keys lost cost?

£699 if the car is open, £799 if it is locked, at the workshop. Mobile visits within 30 miles are £129 extra.

What happens to the lost keys?

Where the system allows, lost keys are removed from the car so they will not start it if someone finds them.

I am selling the car and only have one key. Can you help?

Yes. A second key before sale is a spare key job, £220 at the workshop or £349 with a mobile visit.

Is my model covered?
