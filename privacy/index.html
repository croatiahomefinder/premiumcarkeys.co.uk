Privacy notice

How Kovac Services Ltd, trading as Premium Car Keys, handles information sent through premiumcarkeys.co.uk.

What we collect

When you contact us we may collect your name, phone number, email address, postcode, vehicle details, registration, the service you need, photos you send us and anything else you choose to include. For key work we also check photo ID and proof of ownership.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, quote and carry out the work, confirm that you own or are authorised to use the vehicle, keep job and warranty records, prevent fraud and theft, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

How the price calculator works

The calculator runs in your browser and does not store anything on this website. When you send, it opens WhatsApp or your email app with your details filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send there. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also handled under WhatsApp's own terms.

Third-party services on this site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and show an embedded Google Map. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when these load. This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to carry out the work, operate the website, or comply with the law, including requests from the police about vehicle security.

Retention and your rights

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including job, warranty, accounting and legal records. UK data protection law gives you rights over your personal information. To ask what we hold, request a correction or make another request, email info@kslimited.co.uk.
