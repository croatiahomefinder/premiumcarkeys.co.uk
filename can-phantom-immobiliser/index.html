What it does

Modern cars can be taken without the thief ever touching your key. CAN Phantom adds a step they cannot get round with a copied key: the engine stays disabled until the right sequence is entered.

  • Engine will not run without your own sequence
  • Sequence entered on the car's existing buttons, such as the steering wheel
  • No extra fob, tag or keypad to carry
  • Hidden in the wiring, nothing obvious to find
  • Service and valet modes for garages and handovers

Questions

Does it replace my alarm or tracker?

No. It adds a separate layer that stops the engine starting. Keep your alarm and tracker.

Can a garage still service the car?

Yes. Service and valet modes let you hand the car over without giving out your sequence.

What if I forget my sequence?

There is an override for that situation, and we show you how it works at handover.

Can you fit it at my house?

Yes, within about 30 miles of Halesowen, or book a slot at the workshop.
