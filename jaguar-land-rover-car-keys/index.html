Questions

Do I need to go to a Jaguar or Land Rover dealer?

No. We cut, program and test keys for supported 2010 to 2023 models at our workshop or at your address.

Range Rovers get stolen a lot. What can I do?

A CAN Phantom immobiliser stops the engine starting until your own button sequence is entered, even if a key is copied. It is £399 when fitted with your key work.

Will my existing key keep working?

Yes. A spare key is added alongside the keys you already have, so bring every working key to the appointment.

What happens to the lost keys on an all keys lost job?

Where the system allows, lost keys are removed from the car so they will not start it if someone finds them.

Is my model covered?
